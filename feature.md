At least three or four photos show notebooks or paper documents, but only one appeared. We do not know which specific images are in the 31 failures or why they failed. The user wants photographed handwritten or printed notebook pages/documents flagged when the paper is the main subject, not incidental background paper. Also diagnose the failed analyses with safe aggregate information. Do not assume the model will classify every document correctly from prompt wording alone.

This is a new candidate type, not a change to the saved 50–100% auto-selection threshold. A low-confidence *candidate* still appears unchecked; ordinary, uncertain, and failed images do not appear. Preserve consent, private-LAN-only image requests, no retries, read-only scans, in-memory previews, no private text in explanations/history, and explicit exact-path quarantine confirmation.
